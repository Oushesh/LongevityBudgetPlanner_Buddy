In the German healthcare market, this is achieved by shifting from physical "Point of Care" logistics to "Digital Orchestration" and "Regulatory Arbitrage."

The three most efficient ways to bypass or optimize the physical bottlenecks—while strictly adhering to Anordnungs-, Auswahl-, and Anwesenheitspflicht—are outlined below.

1. B2B Diagnostics-as-a-Service (The "Remi / Probatix" Play)
The Concept: Do not build the clinical or logistics pipeline. Outsource the legal liability and the physical handling to specialized German infrastructure companies like Probatix or Remi Health.

How it bypasses the bottleneck: These platforms have pre-negotiated, API-driven networks with major laboratory chains (Synlab, Limbach, Lademannbogen).

The Legal Hack: They provide the Telearzt (telemedical doctor) who legally issues the lab order (Anordnungsverantwortung). They handle the shipping of the CE-certified sampling kits directly to the consumer’s home.

The Cost: Zero capital expenditure on physical space. You pay a variable fee per processed test kit, keeping your startup asset-light and highly scalable.

2. Dry Capillary Blood Home-Sampling (The "Self-Collection" Loop)
The Concept: Avoid "delegation" entirely by removing the third-party phlebotomist from the equation. If the patient draws their own blood, there is no medical worker to supervise, rendering Auswahl- and Anwesenheitspflicht legally obsolete.

How it works: You mail the user a modern micro-sampling kit (like Neoteryx Mitra or Capitainer). The user pricks their finger at home and collects a precise volume of blood onto a dry polymeric tip.

The Regulatory Loophole: Because the customer performs the prick on themselves, it is legally classified as self-sampling (similar to a diabetic checking blood sugar). No doctor needs to be physically present.

What you can test: While you cannot run a massive 100-biomarker panel on capillary blood, modern labs can accurately extract key biohacking markers like Vitamin D3, HbA1c, Cholesterol profiles, Omega-3 Index, and hs-CRP from just a few dry drops.

3. "Digital-First" Doctor Partnerships (The Hub-and-Spoke MVZ)
The Concept: If you absolutely need venous blood (large vials from the arm for deep testing), do not hire doctors to stand next to MFA-Azubis. Instead, build a digital network with existing private medical practices (Arztpraxen or MVZs).

The Setup: Build a simple partner portal. When a user books a deep panel on your app, they are issued a booking slot at a local, pre-onboarded private GP or private sports-medicine practice in Munich, Berlin, or Hamburg.

Why Doctors Cooperate: Private doctors in Germany love IGeL (out-of-pocket) patients. You act as their marketing engine, driving high-paying, health-conscious private clients directly to their practice.

The Legal Compliance: The partner doctor’s practice handles the blood draw. Because it occurs inside a licensed medical practice with an active doctor in the building, Anordnungs-, Auswahl-, and Anwesenheitspflicht are automatically, 100% fulfilled by the partner's existing infrastructure. You bear zero medical liability.
