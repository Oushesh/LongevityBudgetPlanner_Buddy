DOCUMENTS THAT NEED TO BE SUBMITTED:

Artificial Intelligence: 


Software Lifecycle Process documentation:
Description of SW development processes, applicable Standard Operating Procedures and relevant records of application
to the device under assessment
SW System Safety Classification, based on IEC 62304
SW Development Plan that describes how the software is developed, including processes, deliverables, milestones,
responsibilities, and timelines
SW Requirement Specifications that lists what the software is expected to do, including functional and safety-related
requirements
SW Architecture that outlines the structure of the software and is showing how its components interact
SOUPs (Software of Unknown Provenance), how they are integrated in the SW Architecture, and their known anomalies to
list third-party software used and any known bugs or issues these software components have
SW Detailed Design that describes the internal logic and design of each software component in detail
SW Maintenance Plan to explain how the software will be updated, fixed, and managed after initial release
SW V&V test strategy, test procedures and test records covering all applicable levels (e.g. Unit, Integration, User
Acceptance, System Tests, etc.)
SW traceability (from requirements to tests), including Risk Control Measures
SW Release Document which confirms the software is complete and safe to release, summarizing tests, reviews, approvals
and version control activities
Description of SW deployment method (e.g.: Physical support, App, Webapp, Pre-installed etc.) as part of manufacturing,
including production and final testing tools validation
SW Risk Management, including identification of SW Items contributing to Hazardous Situations, related potential causes of
failure, and Risk Control Measures
Usability Engineering File, including identification of primary operating functions, use error risk analysis, plans and reports for
verification of implementation and validation of effectiveness of all Risk Control Measures related to possible use errors.
